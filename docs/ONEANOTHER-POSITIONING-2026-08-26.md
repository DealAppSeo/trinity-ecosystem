# ONE ANOTHER — one-page positioning

**For the 26 August 2026 "How to Win" session.** Internal working document — nothing public
until the build window opens. The designed version is `docs/oneanother-kit/`.

> **Human generosity. Agentically orchestrated. Verifiably delivered.**
>
> The world does not only have a generosity problem. It has a **coordination** problem.
> Money, skills, time and relationships already exist. They rarely find each other in the
> right combination.

---

## What it is

**ONE ANOTHER turns willingness to help into verified help.** Trusted agents understand a real
burden, assemble the people and resources that can carry it, protect privacy, and return
consequential decisions to human stewards.

> **We don't just raise money. We assemble help.**

**The category is Impact Orchestration.** Not crowdfunding, not volunteer scheduling, not a
referral directory. The north-star metric is **burdens borne together** — not dollars raised,
hours logged, or referrals sent.

---

## The demo, in five beats

Fictional composite. One burden in, one verified outcome out.

1. A single mother's car fails. She types one message, in her own words, to her church.
2. **Listen agent** decomposes it: transportation · employment risk · childcare · financial · relational.
3. **Resource agent** assembles: a verified volunteer mechanic, temporary rides, a corporate Impact Pool, a benevolence grant.
4. **Trust gate** checks credential, eligibility and policy. A **human steward** approves.
5. **Proof of Care** records the outcome — without publishing the person.

**Outcome:** transportation restored · job preserved · childcare bridged · someone showed up.
Not "$550 raised." No single donor, volunteer or nonprofit solved it — they carried it together.

## Why the existing platforms don't cover this

The useful question is not which has better features. It is what each one *optimises* — and
what is left over.

| Category | Optimises | What remains unsolved |
|---|---|---|
| **Crowdfunding** — GoFundMe | Story → audience → money | Requires reach and publicity; rarely assembles non-financial help around the need |
| **Fundraising OS** — Givebutter, BetterWorld | Campaigns, donors, CRM, events | Excellent fundraising rails — but not a cross-resource outcome orchestrator |
| **Volunteer coordination** — SignUpGenius | Slots and scheduling | The opportunity must already be defined by a human before anyone can sign up |
| **Skills volunteering** — Catchafire | Professional-to-nonprofit projects | Matches discrete projects, not a dynamically assembled multi-resource care plan |
| **Corporate purpose** — Benevity | Giving, matching, volunteering, grants | Huge enterprise rails; aligning impact with real nonprofit demand stays hard |
| **Social care** — Findhelp, Unite Us | Service discovery and referral | Coordinates institutional services; does not activate latent human generosity as supply |
| **ONE ANOTHER** | **Verified outcomes** | Builds the solution from money + people + skills + goods + relationships, with trust and proof |

## MVP scope — what must work live

One vertical slice. Seven things, and nothing else.

- Private natural-language intake — no category picker
- Agent decomposition into interdependent needs
- Multi-resource discovery: money + helper + ride + service
- One selective-disclosure credential flow
- A human approval gate that fails closed
- One real action — something actually created, sent, bought or scheduled
- A Proof of Care, verifiable without exposing the person

## Non-negotiable

The lines that make the product defensible — and that we will be asked about.

- **The person receiving help is never the product.** No public marketplace of vulnerable people.
- **Need is not reputation.** Agents, providers and organisations earn scores. People in need are never scored.
- **Public proof never means public hardship.** Proof is about the integrity of the process, not the person.
- **Mercy is not gamified.** Food, shelter and safety never sit behind "complete three tasks to unlock care."
- **Web3 must solve a real cross-organisation trust problem** — or it does not belong in the MVP.
