# AMINA Bank Zurich Pitch Script

*(5-Minute Verbatim Script for Hackathon Finale & AMINA Sandbox Proposal)*

**[0:00] THE HOOK**
"In 2026, the question is no longer *if* AI agents will execute financial transactions, but *how* institutions can trust them to do it. You don't give a junior analyst the keys to the treasury on day one. Why are we doing it with LLMs? I'm here to introduce TrustRails."

**[1:00] THE PROBLEM**
"Right now, Web3 is building programmable money—USDC on Solana—and Agent-Fi is building autonomous bots. But Fireblocks and institutional custodians enforce *human* rules. There is a massive compliance gap when a generative AI agent signs a transaction. If it hallucinates and dumps a million dollars, who is liable?"

**[2:00] THE SOLUTION**
"TrustShell is a modular pre-authorization layer that sits in front of custody. It translates institutional mandates into cryptographic proofs. Before an agent moves money, it undergoes Know Your Agent (KYA). We verify its mathematical Reputation Identity (RepID). We run its proposed action through a Byzantine Fault Tolerant mesh of 5 different LLMs. If they disagree, the transaction is frozen."

**[3:00] LIVE DEMO TRANSITION**
"Let's look at the Institutional Control Matrix... Notice this $75k mock payment. It triggers the dual-authorization threshold we set. The agent receives a 202-accepted hold, and the CTO and CFO are pinged. When approved, a Compliance Receipt is generated and pinned permanently via a Solana Devnet transaction. You can click the Explorer link right there."

**[4:00] THE DIFFERENCE**
"Competitors are focused on hiding human transactions with ZK. TrustRails is focused on proving agent trustworthiness *without exposing the underlying institutional algorithms*. We provide accountability without exposure."

**[4:30] THE ASK**
"We are looking for a 90-day pilot inside the AMINA Bank innovation sandbox to stress test this against Swiss FINMA requirements and European MiCA frameworks. Agents earn trust just like people earn credit. TrustRails is how you measure it. Thank you."
