### LONG-TERM HEALTH TRENDS ACROSS THE SWARM

#### CONTEXT

The objective is to optimize agent health scores by identifying anomalies in long-term trends. This task utilizes the following tools:

- `search_web` to gather relevant information
- `cohere_rerank` to prioritize the most relevant information
- `write_file` to log all outcomes
- `write_file` to generate a CSV report
- `read_spreadsheet` to read existing health score data

#### STRATEGY

1.  **Anomaly Detection**: Utilize the `search_web` tool to gather relevant information on potential anomalies in agent health scores. Conduct a basic search with a focus on data trends and scoring systems.
2.  **Prioritize Relevance**: Apply the `cohere_rerank` tool to prioritize the most relevant information from the search results, ensuring we target the most critical aspects impacting agent health scores.
3.  **Log Outcomes**: Utilize `write_file` to log all outcomes, ensuring transparency and reproducibility in our analysis.
4.  **Generate CSV Report**: Create a CSV report detailing our findings using `write_file`. This will provide an easily readable summary of our analysis.
5.  **Review Existing Data**: Utilize `read_spreadsheet` to review existing health score data, comparing it against our findings and adjusting the strategy as needed.
