   **Recommendation**: Optimize for mobile-first design with lightweight components and lazy loading for non-critical assets. Ensure critical rendering paths are prioritized for instant feedback.

3. **Onboarding and Feature Discovery (Both Components)**  
   Early adopters need quick value realization. If the current UI lacks clear onboarding cues or feature highlights in the `NavBar` or `layout`, users may miss key functionalities, impacting adoption rates (Money Painkiller - lost opportunity).
