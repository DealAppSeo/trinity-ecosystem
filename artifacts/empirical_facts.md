## Empirical Facts

* The pyramids of Giza were built around 2580-2565 BC.
* The Great Pyramid of Giza, built for Pharaoh Khufu, is the largest of the three pyramids and is believed to have taken around 20 years to build.
* The pyramids were built using limestone and granite blocks, some weighing as much as 2.5 tons.
* The ancient Egyptians used simple yet effective tools made of copper to construct the pyramids.
* The pyramids were built as tombs for the pharaohs and were believed to provide a means for them to ascend to the afterlife.
