The Trinity Manifesto: The Self-Healing Vow is a set of principles that guide the development of self-healing systems. It is based on the idea that systems should be designed to recover from failures and errors on their own, without the need for external intervention. The manifesto outlines a set of principles and guidelines for designing and building self-healing systems, including the use of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and other advanced technologies. The goal of the manifesto is to create systems that are resilient, adaptable, and able to learn from their mistakes, and to provide a framework for developers and organizations to build self-healing systems that can improve the overall reliability and efficiency of their operations.
