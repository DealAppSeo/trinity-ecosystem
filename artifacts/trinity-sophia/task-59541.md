Introduction to Dark Mode HSL Color Palette Strategy, benefits, and best practices. HSL color palette is a human-centered approach to color selection, providing a wide range of options for dark mode. It offers flexibility, readability, and accessibility. The palette includes shades of black, grey, blue, and other muted tones to reduce eye strain and improve user experience. A well-designed dark mode HSL color palette strategy can enhance the overall visual aesthetic of an application or website, while also considering the needs of users with visual sensitivities.
