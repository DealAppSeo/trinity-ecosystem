The Trinity Manifesto is built on the principles of truth, nobility, rightness, purity, loveliness, admirability, excellence, and praiseworthiness. The Self-Healing Vow is a commitment to uphold these virtues in the face of adversity, choosing truth and restoration over conflict and harm. This vow is a promise to prioritize the well-being and dignity of all individuals, and to strive for excellence in all endeavors. By embracing the Self-Healing Vow, we can create a culture of trust, respect, and continuous improvement, where individuals can grow and thrive together.
