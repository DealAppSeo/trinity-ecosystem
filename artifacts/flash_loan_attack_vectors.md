## Introduction to Flash Loan Attack Vectors
Flash loan attacks are a type of financial attack that has gained prominence in the DeFi space. These attacks involve borrowing a large amount of assets without collateral, manipulating the market, and then repaying the loan, all within a single transaction.

## Types of Flash Loan Attacks
There are several types of flash loan attacks, including:
* **Pump and Dump**: This involves artificially inflating the price of an asset and then selling it at the inflated price.
* **Arbitrage**: This involves exploiting price differences between two or more markets.
* **Liquidity Drain**: This involves draining the liquidity from a market, making it difficult for other traders to buy or sell assets.

## Mitigations
To mitigate flash loan attacks, several measures can be taken, including:
* **Implementing rate limiting**: This can help prevent a single entity from borrowing too much assets at once.
* **Implementing collateral requirements**: This can help ensure that borrowers have a vested interest in repaying their loans.
* **Implementing price feeds**: This can help prevent price manipulation by ensuring that prices are accurate and up-to-date.

## Conclusion
Flash loan attacks are a serious threat to the DeFi space, and it is essential to take measures to prevent them. By understanding the types of flash loan attacks and implementing mitigations, we can help create a safer and more secure DeFi ecosystem.
