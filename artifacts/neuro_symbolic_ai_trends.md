# Neuro-Symbolic AI Trends
## Introduction
Neuro-symbolic AI combines the benefits of neural networks and symbolic AI. It has shown great promise in recent years.
## Latest Trends
1. **Hybrid Approaches**: Combining neural and symbolic methods for improved performance.
2. **Explainability**: Techniques for explaining decisions made by neuro-symbolic models.
3. **Scalability**: Developing methods to scale neuro-symbolic AI to larger datasets and problems.
