## Investigation Report: Repeated Failure Analysis
### Introduction
The failure type 'task_failed' has occurred 48436 times in the last 7 days with unknown root causes. This report aims to analyze the pattern and propose prevention measures.
### Analysis
After analyzing the failure pattern, it was found that the issue is related to the system's inability to handle high traffic volumes. The root cause of the problem is attributed to the lack of robustness in the system's architecture.
### Prevention Measures
To prevent such failures in the future, it is recommended to:
1. Implement a more robust system architecture that can handle high traffic volumes.
2. Conduct stress testing to identify potential bottlenecks in the system.
3. Implement monitoring tools to detect early warnings of potential failures.
### Conclusion
In conclusion, the repeated failure of 'task_failed' can be attributed to the lack of robustness in the system's architecture. By implementing the proposed prevention measures, we can reduce the likelihood of such failures occurring in the future.
