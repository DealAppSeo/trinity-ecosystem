# Planetary Restart Guide for Railway Swarm

## Introduction
As the orchestrator, it is essential to have a comprehensive guide for manually restarting the entire swarm in Railway. This document outlines the step-by-step process to ensure a smooth and efficient restart.

## Pre-Requisites
Before initiating the restart process, ensure that you have the following:
- Access to the Railway dashboard
- Necessary credentials for the swarm
- Understanding of the swarm's architecture and components

## Step 1: Preparation
Research and gather information on the current state of the swarm, including:
- Number of nodes and their roles
- Current services and deployments
- Any ongoing tasks or processes

## Step 2: Shutdown Sequence
To avoid data corruption or loss, follow this shutdown sequence:
1. **Stop all services**: Disable all services and deployments to prevent any new tasks from being executed.
2. **Drain node connections**: Gradually drain connections from each node to prevent any sudden loss of connectivity.
3. **Terminate tasks**: Terminate all running tasks and processes to ensure a clean shutdown.

## Step 3: Restart Sequence
Once the swarm is shut down, follow this restart sequence:
1. **Restart core nodes**: Initialize the core nodes to ensure the foundation of the swarm is stable.
2. **Restart service nodes**: Bring up the service nodes, followed by the deployment of services.
3. **Restore connections**: Gradually restore connections to each node, ensuring a stable and balanced swarm.

## Step 4: Verification and Monitoring
After the restart, verify that:
- All nodes are online and functional
- Services are deployed and running correctly
- Connections are stable and balanced
Continuously monitor the swarm's performance and adjust as needed to ensure optimal operation.

## Conclusion
By following this guide, you can manually restart the entire swarm in Railway, ensuring a smooth and efficient process. Remember to always prioritize data integrity and swarm stability.
