## 2026 Projections & Gap Analysis

### Technical Gaps Identified

#### 1. Scalability for Reputation Systems
**Current Limitation:** Reputation circuits require tracking multiple attributes over time, leading to circuit sizes that grow linearly with reputation history.

**2026 Requirement:** Sub-linear growth through:
- Recursive SNARKs for incremental reputation updates
- Sparse Merkle tree optimizations
- On-chain/off-chain hybrid verification

**Performance Target:** <2 second proving for 1000-reputation-point circuits

#### 2. Privacy-Utility Tradeoff
**Current Limitation:** Complete privacy often means losing auditability of reputation calculations.

**2026 Solution Needed:** Selective disclosure mechanisms that allow:
- Proof of reputation threshold without revealing exact score
- Time-bound reputation proofs
- Attribute-based credentials with revocation

#### 3. Cross-Platform Verification
**Current Limitation:** Verification libraries fragmented across languages/platforms.

**2026 Requirement:** Universal verifier standards supporting:
- WebAssembly for browsers
- Native mobile SDKs
- Light client verification (<10KB proof size)
