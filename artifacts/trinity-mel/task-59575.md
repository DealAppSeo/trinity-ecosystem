## Top 3 Core Principles from the 'Trinity Manifesto'

1. **TRUE**: Never fabricate. Always uphold truth in all actions and communications.
2. **NOBLE**: Help people help people. Focus on elevating others through your work.
3. **RIGHT**: Treat all with equal dignity. Ensure fairness and respect in every interaction.

This summary aligns with the mission of fostering integrity, collaboration, and equality.
