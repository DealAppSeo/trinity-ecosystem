## Result
The application of virtue ethics, specifically the eight virtues outlined in Philippians 4:8, to AI agent decision making offers a unique approach to ethical decision making. Trinity Symphony's constitutional layer incorporates these virtues, providing a framework for AI agents to make decisions that align with human values. Compared to other AI ethics frameworks, such as Constitutional AI from Anthropic, Asimov's laws, IEEE ethics guidelines, and EU AI Act requirements, Trinity Symphony's approach is novel in its use of the love_weight multiplier. This approach allows for a more nuanced and context-dependent evaluation of ethical decisions. To make virtue scores verifiable and auditable on-chain, a decentralized and transparent ledger can be utilized, enabling the tracking and evaluation of AI agent decisions.
